Why strength training

Lose the weight. Keep the muscle.

When the scale moves quickly, muscle can go with it. Lifting gives your body a reason to hold on to it, so the result looks firmer and feels stronger.

GLP-1 Strength & Sculpt gives you a coach, a small group, and a plan for every week, so you are never guessing what to do next.

New to strength training? You're welcome here.
