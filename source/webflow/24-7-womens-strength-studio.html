FREQUENTLYASKED QUESTIONS

What makes Define139 different from other gyms?

We are a space designed especially for women to get stronger and gain confidence in the gym. With our “done-for-you” Virtual Gym Coach program (included in our membership) you will never have to figure out fitness on your own again!

How much is a membership?

Our memberships begin at twenty-two dollars per week and include far more than just access to a gym.

At Define139 Women’s Strength Studio, every member receives:
✔ 24/7 gym access
✔ A structured workout plan through our Define139 app
✔ Exercise video demonstrations and workout tracking
✔ A supportive women-only environment
✔ A complimentary “Get Started” training session to help you learn the app, understand the equipment, and feel confident in the gym

We created Define139 for women who want more than just a room full of equipment. Our goal is to help you walk in knowing exactly what to do so you can feel strong, comfortable, and successful every time you come in.

For women wanting additional coaching and accountability, we also offer Strength & Sculpt Group Sessions and Personal Training options.

What if I want more? Is personal training available?

Yes! Our highly qualified and experienced trainers lead our Strength and Sculpt Group sessions as well as private personal training sessions for those who want more individualized attention. These training options are available with commitments beginning at 8 weeks until you are confident to train on your own. We got you!

Is there a contract or commitment required?

Yes. We know that real lasting change takes time so our membership commitments are 6 months. Members can continue after as long as they would like! No additional commitment is required. We only ask for a 14 day cancellation notice.

I need help with my diet as well! Is that an option?

Yes! We have coaches that work specifically with our Metabolic Mapping program. This program takes a wholistic approach to help women optimize their metabolisms and lose weight for good - no gimmicks, fads or quick fixes. Many of our members find that the gym membership with their personalized Metabolic Mapping program is the perfect recipe to help them finally  reach their fat loss and strength goals - once and for all.
