Meet Our Staff

Greta Quesenberry
Founder | Metabolic Health Coach | Co-Owner of Define139 Women's Strength Studio

For years, Greta personally struggled with the exhausting cycle of yo-yo dieting, chasing quick fixes, and feeling frustrated by a body that no longer seemed to respond the way it once did. Like many women, she spent years believing the answer was simply more restriction, more cardio, and more discipline—only to find herself feeling defeated, exhausted, and stuck.

That journey ultimately became the foundation for the work she does today. After learning how chronic dieting impacts metabolism, hormones, energy, and overall health, Greta became passionate about helping women step away from extremes and build a healthier, more sustainable approach to fitness and fat loss. She created Metabolic Mapping to help women restore metabolic function, rebuild confidence, and develop healthy habits rooted in nourishment, strength, and consistency instead of guilt and punishment.

In 2025, Greta and her husband Greg opened Define139 Women's Strength Studio after carrying a dream since the 1990s of owning a gym together. Today, Define139 is more than a gym to Greta—it's a place where women can belong, feel supported, and discover they are capable of becoming stronger physically, mentally, and spiritually than they ever imagined.

When she's not coaching, Greta enjoys strength training with her husband and kiddos, reading, and hiking with the family dogs.

Greg Quesenberry
Co-Owner | Gym Manager | Head Trainer

Greg Quesenberry is the co-owner of Define139 Women's Strength Studio and serves as the gym manager and head trainer. With a strong background in exercise science and years of hands-on experience in strength training, Greg is passionate about helping people build strength, move better, and gain confidence both inside and outside the gym.

Greg holds a Bachelor of Science in Exercise and Sports Science and brings a practical, results-driven approach to training. He believes fitness should be sustainable, effective, and approachable—whether someone is brand new to the gym or looking to take their training to the next level.

At Define139, Greg plays a key role in creating a welcoming environment where women feel comfortable learning proper form, understanding equipment, and building strength safely and confidently. His coaching style focuses on encouragement, consistency, and helping clients develop long-term habits that support overall health and performance.

When he's not coaching or managing the gym, Greg enjoys strength training with Greta, fishing, and spending time with his family.

Her coaching style focuses on balance, consistency, and creating realistic habits that support long-term success. Sophie is passionate about helping women feel strong in their own skin and become the healthiest version of themselves without feeling trapped in an "all or nothing" mindset.
