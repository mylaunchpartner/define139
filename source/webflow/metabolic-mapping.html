FREQUENTLYASKED QUESTIONS

What makes Define139 different from other fitness programs?

We combine faith, science, and sustainability to help you transform your health without extremes. No fads or guilt—just grace, grit, and results that last.

What’s included in Metabolic Mapping?

Strength training, protein-rich nutrition guidance, daily movement goals, nervous system support, and a mindset rooted in Scripture and science.

Are personal training sessions included in all of the plans?

One-on-one personal training is available through select coaching tiers, but not included in every plan. Most programs offer guided workouts, video demos, and expert support—designed to be flexible and empowering whether you train at home or in our studio. If you’re looking for personalized training, just let us know. We’ll help you choose the best fit for your goals.

What’s the difference between the gym membership and online coaching?

Our Women’s Strength Studio in Green Valley, WV offers in-person access to strength equipment, community workouts, and optional coaching add-ons. It’s perfect if you live nearby and want hands-on support.

Our online coaching gives you structured workouts, metabolic guidance, and mindset support—all from home. You’ll still get personalized feedback and a plan tailored to your body, schedule, and faith journey.

Is there a contract or commitment required?

Yes—to give your body time to reset and results time to stick, all plans come with a commitment.

You can choose a 6-month or 12-month gym membership, with extra savings when you lock in your rate for the year!

All online-only coaching plans also require a minimum 6-month commitment. We believe real change takes time—and you're worth that investment.

How do I cancel my membership?

Depending on your plan type, you may need to give written notice up to 60 days. Refer to your contract for exact terms, or message us, and we would be happy to help find the answer for your specific plan type.
